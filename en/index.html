About us

Tailored consulting for the Scandinavian market

CONSULTINGDK provides professional, specialized consulting for companies looking to expand their business beyond their home country's borders. Through quality services, we guide the entry into the Danish and Scandinavian market from the very first stage, with careful, precise market analysis to design and implement the strategy that best fits each case.

Our added value

An exclusive network and high-profile events

CONSULTINGDK's strength lies in offering access to strategic exhibition spaces and connecting our clients to a fast-growing network, including through exclusive events organized in partnership with local institutions. A deep knowledge of the market allows us to build sales networks tailored to every need.
