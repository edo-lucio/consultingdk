What we do

Tailored representation to enter the Danish market

CONSULTINGDK provides professional, specialized consulting for companies looking to expand their business beyond their home country's borders. Through quality services, we guide the entry into the Danish and Scandinavian (European) market from the very first stage, carrying out careful, precise market analysis to design and implement the perfect strategy for each specific case.

Our strength lies in connecting our clients to an exclusive, fast-growing network, including through high-profile events organized in partnership with local institutions. Specialized in building sales networks, we draw on a deep knowledge of the local market and exclusive resources suited to any need.
