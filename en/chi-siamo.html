A bit about us

A 360-degree solution for entering Scandinavia

We are a team with proven, long-standing experience in the Danish market. We have built commercial and institutional relationships with leading players across the many industries and businesses we work with.

For this reason, we can offer a full 360-degree solution for everything involved in bringing Italian businesses into the Scandinavian market — from the initial market analysis to the organization of promotional events on the ground.
