Agrifarm Tour

A different, natural kind of holiday: the first exhibition to bring the world of Italian agriturismo to Scandinavia, with relaxation, authentic flavors and unforgettable landscapes.

The event

A new exhibition dedicated to Italian agriturismo

"Agrifarm Tour" (from the English farmstay, the term used in the English-speaking world for farm holidays) is an exhibition that presents the world of Italian agriturismo to the Scandinavian public. It was born from an idea by Paolo Dorati, CEO of ConsultingDK, with the goal of bringing this rural tourism experience to Scandinavia for the first time.

Agrifarm is a form of tourism where travelers visit one or more working farms to enjoy the landscape and discover up close — with a taste — how excellent products like wine, cheese and other local specialties are made, and to live an experience in close contact with nature.

The common thread is always the same: a holiday in nature.

The experience

What you can do at an agriturismo

Relax in nature

Enjoy stunning countryside landscapes, far from the chaos of the city, immersed in the tranquility of the Italian countryside.

Tastings & wine tours

Taste the farm's typical products or join a guided tour through vineyards and cellars.

Walking, cycling and horseback riding

Explore the countryside in complete tranquility on foot, by bike or on horseback, with unforgettable excursions.

History & local heritage

Discover the history of the farm and the surrounding area, and much more, guided by those who live it every day.

Cheese & cured meat tastings

Discover the local dairy and charcuterie excellence, told and served directly by those who make it.

Cooking classes & taste workshops

Try your hand at traditional recipes, guided by those who keep time-honored techniques alive.

Organization

An event built with outstanding partners

ConsultingDK, in partnership with Italien i Danmark, organizes the first edition of "Agrifarm Tour" in the charming setting of the city of Copenhagen.
