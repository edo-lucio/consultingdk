Simply Haring Collection

Keith Haring's art meets new spaces and new audiences: a private collection dedicated to Keith Haring and the communicative power of his art.

The collection

A private collection dedicated to Keith Haring

The Simply Haring Collection is a private collection dedicated to Keith Haring and the communicative power of his art. It brings together prints, lithographs, editions, multiples and posters that capture the vitality of an artist able to take art outside traditional spaces and make it accessible to everyone.

Through exhibitions and collaborations, the collection travels through museums, exhibition spaces, workplaces and public venues. Each project is designed to enter into dialogue with the place that hosts it and the people who experience it.

The proposal

A collection to share

Haring's work speaks of energy, movement, community and participation. His images have crossed galleries and museums, but also streets, clubs and everyday spaces. The Simply Haring Collection gathers this spirit and offers it in flexible exhibition formats, designed for different contexts and audiences.

The collection can be presented through a dedicated curatorial path, a thematic selection or a display designed together with the host venue.

Projects for museums and exhibition spaces

Museums, foundations, galleries and cultural spaces can work with the collection to create temporary exhibitions, thematic routes and public events. Each proposal is developed around the spaces available, the duration of the project and the target audience.

The project can include the selection of works, the exhibition concept, accompanying texts and the coordination of activities connected to the exhibition.

Projects for companies and meeting places

The collection gives companies the opportunity to bring art into workplaces, hotels, commercial spaces, showrooms and venues open to the public. An exhibition can become part of a place's identity, offering clients, staff and visitors a recognisable cultural experience.

Collaborations can take different forms: temporary exhibitions, private events, initiatives for employees and clients, or projects integrated into a company's communication and programming.

Co-marketing and collaborations

Each project can be accompanied by shared communication activities between the collection and the partner: presentations, meetings, special openings, editorial content and initiatives on digital channels.

The goal is to create a cultural collaboration that gives visibility to both the collection and the host venue, and that invites new people to discover Haring's art.

The Simply Haring Collection in some of the spaces that have hosted it: commercial spaces, Museo Permanente in Milan, Moco Museum in London and the Brandhorst Museum in Munich.

A tailor-made project

Designed together with each host venue

Duration, works, display and communication activities are agreed with each partner. The Simply Haring Collection is available to consider proposals across Europe and abroad, with museums, exhibition spaces and companies interested in developing a project together.
