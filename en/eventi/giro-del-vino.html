Giro (dei Vini e del cibo) d'Italia

Italian food & wine culture takes center stage in the heart of Copenhagen: producers, importers and enthusiasts of Italian wine and food products meet for one elegant day.

Register as an exhibitor See the program
The event

A well-established date in Copenhagen and beyond

«Giro (dei Vini e del cibo) d'Italia» is a one-day B2B and B2C event, now in its third edition, hosted at a venue still to be confirmed. It was born from an idea by Paolo Dorati (CEO of ConsultingDK) in partnership with Danitacom.org.

The event brings together individual producers, consortia and groups of companies from the food & wine sector from all over Italy, connecting them directly with Danish professionals, importers, distributors and restaurateurs, as well as with enthusiasts and experts of Italian food & wine.

3rd edition Copenhagen Date to be announced — Spring/Summer 2027
The future of «Giro»

Next goal: more European capitals

After three well-established editions in Copenhagen, we're working to bring «Giro (dei Vini e del cibo) d'Italia» to other Northern European cities too, replicating the same B2B and B2C formula that has made the event a reference point for Italian food & wine in Scandinavia.

Our goal is to build, edition after edition, a circuit of events able to give Italian producers direct access to more Scandinavian and European markets through a single, coordinated event.

Next stops in Northern Europe
Partnership

Italian Chamber of Commerce in Denmark

Operating since 2010 with headquarters in Copenhagen and recognized by Italy's Ministry of Economic Development, the Italian Chamber of Commerce in Denmark works to strengthen commercial relations between Italy and Denmark through business consulting and the organization of events and promotional missions.

ConsultingDK was founded to promote "Made in Italy" wine, Italian food products and tourism, organizing events, promotions and trips between the two countries.

2021 & 2022 Winner

Nebbiolo Winebar

Nebbiolo Winebar is an Italian wine bar and specialty food shop in the heart of Copenhagen, on Store Strandstræde 18, entirely dedicated to Italian wine: a wide regional selection served by the glass and by the bottle, paired with antipasti, plus tastings and custom events for private guests and companies.

For two consecutive editions, 2021 and 2022, Nebbiolo Winebar won the Giro (dei Vini e del cibo) d'Italia award, confirming its place as one of the key references for Italian wine in Denmark — and the best winebar in Copenhagen.
