What we do

Catering and events, end to end

Together with Dorati Catering we offer a full catering and event organization service for the Danish market. We create your private parties, corporate events, julefrokost, and tailor-made lunches and dinners, taking care of every detail from the kitchen to the floor service.

A team of experienced professionals is by your side at every stage of the organization: from choosing the menu to floor staff, from transport to logistics, all the way to equipment storage, to guarantee a flawless event from start to finish.
